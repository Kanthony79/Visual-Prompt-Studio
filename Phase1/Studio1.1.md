# The Classroom of Tomorrow

## Poster Prompt

Create a promotional science-fiction movie poster titled “The Classroom of Tomorrow.” Show a futuristic Pre-K classroom where a caring teacher and friendly robots help young children learn. Place the teacher in the center as the main focal point, with children and robots around her. Use straight directional lines from glowing computer screens to guide attention toward the teacher and curved lines around the children and robots to create movement. Include circular tables, rectangular screens, and large windows. Use open negative space around the movie title so it is easy to read. Give the robots smooth metallic textures and make them look solid and three-dimensional. Use bright blue and silver colors against deep shadows to create strong contrast and make the teacher stand out.

## Second Prompt Adjustment

Create a second version of this poster. Keep everything else the same, but change the color palette to warm orange, yellow, and white to create a cheerful and hopeful mood.

## Vocabulary Breakdown

Lines: In art a line is the most important building block. In my poster, the bright lines on the ceiling point toward the teacher,
Shape: Shapes are everywhere. The computer screen has four sides, two long and two short sides.

Texture: Texture is how something feels. In my poster, the robot's head looks smooth and shiny.

Volume: Volume means something looks solid and rounded. In my poster, the robot's head looks like a ball.

Contrast: Contrast is putting two different things together to make them stand out.  The yellow and orange colors shout loudly to our eyes.
