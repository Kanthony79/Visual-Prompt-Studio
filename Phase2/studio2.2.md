Visual Prompt Studio 2.2: The Media Rebrand and Technology Evolution Challenge

## Mona Lisa Skincare Advertisement

Create a modern skincare advertisement inspired by Mona Lisa. Show the Mona Lisa holding a luxury face cream called "Mona Glow." Use the elegant gold and soft colors with the slogan " Timeless Beauty, Modern Glow." Combine the classic Renaissance painting style with a polished modern beauty advertisement. Make product name and slogan easy to read.
