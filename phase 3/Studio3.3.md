# Studio 3.3: Tradition and Transformation

## Exhibition Details

- **Title:** Tradition and Transformation
- **Subtitle:** A Global Art Exhibition
- **Dates:** April 12–August 31, 2027
- **Artwork:** Promotional landing-page image created with Microsoft Copilot

## Visual Prompt

Create a promotional landing-page image for a global art exhibition titled “Tradition and Transformation.” Combine Renaissance Naturalism with the modern value of identity and self-expression. Show a diverse group of people expressing their identities through clothing, pose, and color in a museum setting. Use a Pop Art style with bright colors. Include clear lines around the figures and directional lines that guide the viewer’s eyes toward one main focal person. Use strong contrast between deep blue and golden yellow. Leave clear space for the exhibition title and event details.

## Glossary

- **Contemporary Art:** Art connected to ideas and experiences of the present.
- **Pop Art:** An art style that uses bold colors, strong outlines, and imagery from popular culture.
- **Naturalism:** Showing people and objects in a realistic way.
- **Focal Point:** The main part of an artwork that first attracts the viewer’s attention.
- **Directional Lines:** Lines or shapes that guide the viewer’s eyes through an artwork.

## Reflection

I chose the title “Tradition and Transformation” because the exhibition connects older art traditions with ideas that matter today. I used Renaissance Naturalism as inspiration for the figures and museum setting, while asking Copilot to give the image a bright Pop Art look. The deep blue and golden yellow contrast helps the poster stand out. I made the central person in a golden outfit the focal point, and the lines around the figures help lead the viewer’s eyes toward them.

The different clothing, poses, and appearances show identity and self-expression. I wanted the group to suggest that people bring their own stories and backgrounds into a shared museum space. I also asked for clear space for the title and event information so the image could work as a promotional landing-page design. After the first image was made, I asked Copilot to add the exhibition title and details, then changed the dates to April 12–August 31, 2027. This process showed me that a detailed prompt and follow-up edits can help shape an AI-generated design.
