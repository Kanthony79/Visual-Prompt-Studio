# Visual Prompt Studio Assignment 3.1

## Prompt 1 
**Historical theme:** Art connected to survival, ritual, and belief
**Historical period:** Paleolithic
**Artwork inspiration:** Cave Painting, Pech Merle

**Visual prompt:** Create a Paleolithic cave painting inspired by the cave painting at Pech Merle. Show horses painted on a rough limestone cave wall using simple black outlines, black spots, red and black natural pigments, and several handprints. Use texture and a balanced arrangement of the animals. The painting should show the importance of animals for survival and suggest a ritual or spiritual purpose. Do not include anything modern.

## Prompt 2
**Historical theme:** Art as religious instruction for people who could not read 
**Historical period:** Romanesque, Middle Ages
**Artwork inspiration:** Tympanum of the West Portal of Sainte-Foy

**Visual prompt:** Create a Romanesque stone carving inspired by the West Portal tympanum of Sainte-Foy at Conques. Show Christ as the largest figure in the center of a Last Judgment scene, with smaller figures arranged in clear rows around him. Use firm outlines, repeated shapes, balanced groupings, emphasis, and scale to make the religious story easy to understand. Place the carving above a medieval church doorway where pilgrims can see it. The image should show the church teaching Christian beliefs to people who could not read.  Do not include anything modern.
